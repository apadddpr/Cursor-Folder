# Topic 4 · Differentiator Practice System
## From Cursor Challenge Prep Doc · Not script-dependent

**Goal:** Hear a specific issue → know which differentiator to lead with → say it in one sentence.

**Not the goal:** Memorize feature lists or win on benchmarks.

---

## The 4 differentiators (memorize these 4 names only)

| # | Name | One sentence |
|---|------|--------------|
| 1 | **Model neutrality** | Best model changes; Cursor lets you switch without leaving your workflow. |
| 2 | **Large codebase performance** | Cursor finds and uses context across the whole repo, not just the open file. |
| 3 | **Faster time to value** | Works out of the box; DevEx can standardize without every dev building their own setup. |
| 4 | **Platform, not just a tool** | Plan, write, review, iterate in one place (Bugbot, Agent Review, Automations). |

**Cursor wins on:** workflow, context, and execution.

|-------------|------------|

## 10-MINUTE DAILY DRILL (do this 3 days before interview)

### Drill A · Contrast pairs (2 min)
Say out loud, no notes:
- "Copilot: local suggestions. Cursor: ___."
- "Claude Code: terminal agent. Cursor: ___."
(Fill: codebase context, platform, model neutrality, time to value)

### Drill B · Issue → wedge (5 min)
Partner or solo. Random issue from LISTEN table. You have 10 seconds to name:
1. Differentiator (#1–4)
2. One sentence response

**Example:**
- Issue: "Only our staff engineers use Claude Code."
- You: "**Time to value.** Cursor gives the whole org a strong baseline, not just terminal power users."

### Drill C · No script role play (3 min)
Pick Copilot OR Claude. Say only:
1. Acknowledge strength (1 sentence)
2. Ask one discovery question
3. Map their fake answer to one differentiator

No quotes from teleprompter. If you can do this, you are not living by the script.

## FLASHCARD DECK (cut along lines or use Anki)

**FRONT:** Joe says "Copilot handles 80% of what we need."  
**BACK:** Acknowledge autocomplete. Lead **large codebase**: task completion on Central-style multi-file work. Ask where it thins out.

**FRONT:** Joe says "We standardized on Claude."  
**BACK:** Do not attack Claude. Lead **model neutrality**: Cursor runs Claude. Wedge is **platform** + **time to value** for whole org.

**FRONT:** Joe says "Engineers paste into ChatGPT."  
**BACK:** **Large codebase** (no paste) + **Platform** (IDE) + Branden / Privacy Mode.

**FRONT:** Joe says "Review is the bottleneck."  
**BACK:** **Platform**: Bugbot, Agent Review. Not generation, iteration.

**FRONT:** Joe says "We can't standardize, autonomy culture."  
**BACK:** **Time to value**: guardrails via Rules, not mandating prompts. DevEx sets once.

**FRONT:** Joe says "Best model keeps changing."  
**BACK:** **Model neutrality**: optionality without switching tools.

**FRONT:** Joe says "Our monolith is too big for AI."  
**BACK:** **Large codebase**: Semantic Search, Dynamic Context Retrieval. Not wrapping a model.

**FRONT:** Joe says "Copilot Enterprise passed security."  
**BACK:** Acknowledge. **Platform** wedge on workflow. Privacy Mode for Branden if eval expands. Do not fight security.
