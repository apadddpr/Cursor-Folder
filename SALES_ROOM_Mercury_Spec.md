# Mercury Sales Room · Structure & Intro Video Script

**Audience:** Ashwin Baskaran (primary) + Matt, Lev, Branden, Max — anyone Mercury forwards the link to.  
**Not for:** Joe's evaluation of your internal prep (that stays slide 23 / talk track).  
**Take-home walk:** ~60–90 sec tab switch before Topic 4 role play. Room should also stand alone if Ashwin opens it async.

---

## Design principle

The sales room is **aligned signal + shared context**, not homework.

| Include | Exclude |
|---------|---------|
| What Matt & Lev flagged (validated themes) | MEDDPICC worksheets, scoring, cadence tables |
| Hypothesis framed as questions | "We know you use Ada / Copilot / X" |
| Peer proof (Brex, OnePay) | Full competitive battlecards |
| Four Cursor differentiators (prep doc) | Your outbound email copy |
| Security for fintech (Branden) | Internal org research dumps |

Tone: **consultative, warm, peer-level.** Written to Ashwin's team, not to Joe.

---

## Recommended sections (in order)

### 1 · Welcome — What is this room?
**Purpose:** Orient in 10 seconds. Not a generic vendor portal.

**Include:**
- One line: *"A shared space for Mercury as you explore engineering productivity and AI — built from conversations with your team."*
- Who it's for: VP Eng, eng leaders, DevEx / AI Enablement, Security
- What it is / isn't: context and resources, not a contract or product dump
- Your name + one-line credibility (optional)

**Skip:** Long Cursor company history, stock photos, "digital sales room" jargon.

---

### 2 · Intro video (~60–90 sec)
**Purpose:** Human face before the role play / async open. See script below.

---

### 3 · Mercury's moment (Why this conversation)
**Purpose:** Show you did homework **without** assuming their stack.

**Include (sourced, brief):**
- Central integration → codebase complexity inflection
- AI Enablement → moving from siloed experiments to shared infrastructure
- One line: *"We're here to validate whether this matches your priorities — not to tell you what you're already using."*

**Skip:** Ada as headline. Scoring matrix. Job post quotes wall.

---

### 4 · What we heard from your team ★ (hero section)
**Purpose:** Core of the sales room. This is what Joe wants to see vs slide 23 prep.

**Two columns or cards:**

| Matt · AI Enablement | Lev · Banking |
|----------------------|---------------|
| Theme: AI usage is active, but **standardizing across squads** is the harder problem — especially with Central integration | Theme: Same pattern from a **velocity / onboarding** lens as the codebase grows |
| Framed as: shared standards vs team-by-team experiments | Framed as: inconsistency vs onboarding drag |

**Below both:**
> *"From your seat — does this match what you're seeing, or is it different?"*

**Skip:** Verbatim call notes, LinkedIn stalking, "Matt said X on Tuesday."

---

### 5 · What we're exploring together (hypothesis, not pitch)
**Purpose:** Cursor solve as **shared question**, discovery-safe.

**Include (3 bullets max):**
- Can Mercury standardize AI-assisted development across squads during Central integration?
- Can eng get repo-aware, task-level help without every squad inventing its own stack?
- What would a low-risk way to test that look like (e.g. one squad, two weeks, clear metrics)?

Label: **Hypothesis — to validate in conversation**, not "Here's what you need."

---

### 6 · What is Cursor? (Four differentiators — prep doc)
**Purpose:** High-level product frame for anyone new (Max, Branden, forward recipients).

Use prep doc order, Mercury-relevant one-liner each:

| # | Differentiator | Mercury-relevant line |
|---|----------------|----------------------|
| 1 | **Model neutrality** | Use the best model as the landscape shifts — without rebuilding workflow each time |
| 2 | **Large codebase performance** | Context across the whole repo matters when Central adds complexity |
| 3 | **Faster time to value** | DevEx / AI Enablement can set standards once; not every engineer builds their own setup |
| 4 | **Platform, not just a tool** | Plan, write, review, and iterate in one environment — not autocomplete-only |

**One-liner under header:**  
*"Cursor pairs frontier models with better workflow, context, and an integrated way to build software."*

**Skip:** Composer benchmarks, feature laundry list, comparison tables vs Copilot (save for live call).

## Intro video script (~75 sec)

**Format:** You on camera, or voiceover over simple Mercury-neutral slides (no confidential data). Conversational, not corporate.

---

**[0:00 – 0:10 · Hook]**

> Hi Ashwin — I'm Alex, and I work with Cursor. Thanks for taking a look at this room.

**[0:10 – 0:25 · What this is]**

> This isn't a pitch deck and it isn't homework on Mercury. It's a shared space I put together after a few conversations with people on your team — so when we talk, we're starting from the same page, not from a cold outbound email.

**[0:25 – 0:45 · What we heard]**

> Matt on AI Enablement and Lev on Banking surfaced a consistent theme: AI usage is active, but getting it consistent across squads is the harder problem — especially with Central integration underway. I want to validate that with you, not assume it.

**[0:45 – 1:00 · Why Cursor, briefly]**

> Cursor helps fintech teams like Brex and OnePay standardize AI-assisted development across complex codebases — model choice, repo context, and one environment for the full dev cycle, not just autocomplete.

**[1:00 – 1:15 · Close]**

> You'll see peer stories, a short overview of how we think about the problem, and resources if you want to dig in. When we meet, I'd love most of the time on your priorities — and we'll agree on a clear next step either way. Looking forward to it.

---

**Production notes:**
- Total: ~75 sec spoken (~130 words at pace)
- No "I'm not trying to sell you"
- No Ada, no assumed stack
- Record landscape, clean background, captions on
