# Doc F · Discovery Pivot · Internal tools / Ada
## Mercury · Joe as Ashwin

**Open when:** "We built Ada" / "internal Slack bot" / "our own tooling" / references Mercury OSS

**Critical:** Ada proves they care about codebase context. Do NOT attack it.

---

## What Ada signals (say this first)

- Mercury **publicly identified a context gap** between ChatGPT and Copilot-style tools
- They invested in **understanding the codebase**, not just completion
- Likely serves **specific workflows** (e.g. Slack), not every engineer's IDE
- Smart team; this is **build vs buy** conversation, not dismissal

**Say:** "Ada tells me your team already felt the context gap. That aligns with what we hear from other sophisticated eng orgs."

---

## Discovery before you pivot (essential)

1. "Who uses Ada today, and for what workflows?"
2. "Does every engineer get that context, or a subset?"
3. "Is Ada the standard, or one tool among many?"
4. "Does DevEx want to extend Ada, or standardize an IDE layer?"
5. "How does Ada fit with Central integration work?"

---

## Cursor angle (talk track)

**30-second version:**
> "Ada solved a real problem: Mercury-specific context. The question Ashwin usually cares about is whether every engineer gets that value in their daily IDE workflow, or only people in certain channels. Cursor is not a replacement for Ada on day one. It is the org-wide workspace layer with codebase indexing, Rules, and agents built in."

> "Faire built an in-house system called Samurai, then moved agentic work to Cursor when they needed fleet-scale migrations. The pattern is: internal tools for bespoke workflows, plus a standard IDE for the whole org. With AI Enablement hiring and Central integrating, DevEx usually needs both: keep what works internally, standardize how 58 engineers ship code daily."
