**FAQ**

**Do we have to replace what engineers use today?**  
No. Most teams start with a scoped pilot on hard workflows — Central integration, cross-repo work — and compare outcomes. Discovery tells us what to test.

**How is this different from Copilot, Claude Code, or ChatGPT?**  
Happy to go deep in conversation. At a high level: Cursor is built for task completion and repo context across the full dev cycle — with model choice and org-wide standardization — not single-line autocomplete or a separate chat window.

**What about code leaving our environment?**  
Privacy Mode and SOC 2 are documented publicly. See trust.cursor.com. We align with InfoSec early in fintech.

**Who usually owns a rollout?**  
Often AI Enablement or DevEx sets the standard; VP Eng sponsors; InfoSec clears governance; CTO signs off when resourced.

**What does a pilot look like?**  
One squad, ~2 weeks, metrics agreed upfront — velocity, adoption, quality on a real workflow (e.g. integration work).

**Why reach out now?**  
Central integration and the AI Enablement hire suggest a move from siloed experiments to shared infrastructure. Brex and OnePay faced a similar moment in fintech.
