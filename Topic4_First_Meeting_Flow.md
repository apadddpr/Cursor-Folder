# Topic 4 · First Meeting Role Play (Simulated 45-min call)
## Discovery → demo bridge · No actual demo in take-home

---

## Two layers (do not confuse)

| Layer | Reality |
|-------|---------|
| **Take-home presentation** | 60 min, Topics 1–5, **no product demo performed** |
| **Simulated first call (Topic 4)** | You pretend this is a **45-min booked meeting** with Ashwin. Joe evaluates your **standard first-meeting process**, including how you **bridge from disco to demo** |

Joe wants to see your outbound/first-call motion. The demo bridge is part of that even if you never screen-share Cursor.

---

## Simulated 45-min meeting frame (say in upfront contract)

> "Ashwin, thanks for making the time. We have about 45 minutes.
>
> Here is my plan. I will spend the first part understanding your priorities and how the team works today, especially with Central integration. If what I hear sounds like areas where Cursor can help, I would like to use the second part to walk you through how we address those specifically, not a generic product tour.
>
> By the end, we will know if this is worth going deeper, and we will agree on a clear next step either way.
>
> Does that work?"

**Pause. Get yes.**

This sets disco first, demo conditional, next step always. That is your standard process.

---

## Timing inside the simulated call

| Phase | Simulated call | In take-home role play (~18–22 min) |
|-------|----------------|-------------------------------------|
| Open + level-set | 0–5 min | 3–4 min |
| Discovery | 5–20 min | 10–12 min |
| Summarize A/B/C | 20–22 min | 1–2 min |
| **Demo bridge** | 22–24 min | **1–2 min · must show this** |
| Demo (if live) | 24–40 min | **Outline only, 60–90 sec** |
| Next step | 40–45 min | 30 sec |

In the take-home you compress time but **still verbalize the bridge**.

---

## Discovery (unchanged)

10 questions max. Handoff-quality. See Discovery Run Sheet.

Competitive pivot when Joe reveals stack. Prep-doc differentiators only.

---

## Summarize (before bridge)

> "Ashwin, let me make sure I have this right.
>
> You said **[A]**, **[B]**, and **[C]**.
>
> If I am hearing you correctly, the issue is not whether AI is useful. It is **[one-line synthesis, e.g. standardizing across squads on a complex codebase as Central lands]**.
>
> Did I miss anything?"

**Pause.**

---

## Demo bridge (THIS is what Joe wants to see)

Only use if disco surfaced real pain. If Joe said "we are fine," skip to soft next step.

> "Based on what you shared, those are exactly the areas I would want to **show** you, not tell you.
>
> We still have time in our 45 minutes. Rather than a generic tour, I would like to walk you through how Cursor handles **[A, B, C]** in an environment like yours. You can tell me if it is relevant.
>
> Does that work?"

**Pause. Get yes.**

That is the bridge. Do not skip it in the role play.

---

## After the bridge (take-home: no actual demo)

Pick one approach. Rehearse A + B.

### Option A · Narrate the demo plan (60–90 sec, then stop)

> "Great. For your three areas, I would typically show three things:
>
> One, **[A: e.g. standardization]**: how DevEx sets Rules once and every squad inherits the same guardrails.
>
> Two, **[B: e.g. cross-file / Central work]**: how Agent handles a multi-file change with codebase context, not just the open file.
>
> Three, **[C: e.g. review / fintech]**: how eng keeps PR review in the loop, and how Privacy Mode works for teams like yours.
>
> Joe, in a live call I would screen-share here. Happy to take feedback on the discovery and bridge."

### Option B · Joe cuts in after bridge

If Joe stops you at "Does that work?" and says yes, break character:

> "Joe, from here I would run a tailored demo mapped to A, B, and C. I can outline the three beats or move to feedback."

Either is fine. **The bridge must be spoken in character.**

---

## Close after demo would happen (optional, 30 sec)

If you have time after narrating demo plan:

> "Based on what we covered today, a logical next step would be **[technical session with Matt's DevEx team / Branden security sync / scoped eval conversation]**. What works on your end?"

Pilot is not the goal of first meeting. Next step is.

---

## Map A/B/C → what you would show (for bridge + narrate)

| Pain they give you | What you'd demo (say out loud, don't show) |
|--------------------|--------------------------------------------|
| Inconsistent AI / no standard | Rules · one standard for DevEx |
| Cross-file / Central / monolith | Agent · multi-file with codebase context |
| Onboarding / context gap | Ask / codebase-aware workflow |
| Review bottleneck | Review before merge · Bugbot mention if relevant |
| Security / fintech | Privacy Mode one-liner · PR process unchanged |
| Already on Copilot/Claude | Show gap workflow on **their** A/B/C, not a comparison deck |

## Say to Joe before role play starts

> "Joe, I will run this as a first 45-minute call with you as Ashwin. Discovery first. If we find areas Cursor can help, I will bridge to a demo. I will not screen-share the product in the take-home, but I will show you exactly how I would transition."

That sets expectations cleanly.
